About Me

Hi! My name is Anna, and i am a 14 year-old student from the Isle of Man. I created this website to share some of my favorite hobbies and the things I love doing in my free time. I’m creative and a curious person who enjoys exploring new ideas, learning new skills, and having fun while doing it. Whether I’m singing, dancing, acting or even playing the piano, I always find joy in the little things. Thanks for visiting my page— I hope you enjoy learning more about me!

What I do

My singing journey began as a simple love for music, humming melodies and mimicking songs that moved me. Over time, that passion grew into a deeper calling — one that pushed me to find my voice, train it, and share it with the world. From small performances to studio recordings, every step has been a reflection of my growth, emotion, and dedication. Singing isn't just something I do — it's who I am.

Playing the piano fills me with a sense of joy and peace that nothing else can quite match. It’s my way of expressing emotions that words can’t capture—whether it’s happiness, sadness, or just the calm of a quiet moment. I love the feeling of my fingers gliding over the keys, bringing melodies to life and creating something beautiful from silence. The piano is more than an instrument to me; it’s a part of who I am.

I absolutely love to dance—it's how I express myself and have the most fun! I train in ballet, tap, and jazz, and I also enjoy performing in musical theatre. Each style lets me tell a different kind of story through movement, whether it's the grace of ballet, the rhythm of tap, or the energy of jazz. Dance is a huge part of who I am, and I’m always excited to keep learning and growing as a performer.
